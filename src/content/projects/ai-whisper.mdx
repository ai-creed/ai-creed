## what it does

ai-whisper pairs two coding agents — Claude and Codex — in your terminal. They share a single baton — one owns the turn at a time — and structured workflows drive the collaboration, with an evaluator gating each round. The relay isn't tied to any one arrangement; today's workflows pair an implementer and a reviewer (spec-driven-development, ralph-loop).

## why

Hard agent tasks go better with a second agent reviewing. Whisper makes that pairing feel like one workflow instead of two terminals pretending to talk to each other.
